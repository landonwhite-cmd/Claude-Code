# Bar Sign Brainstorm — Movie Quotes

Working list for the Etsy shop. Goal: pick ~8–12 finalists for the launch collection.

## Legend
- **IP risk**: 🟢 low / 🟡 medium / 🔴 high
  - 🟢 = short generic-sounding line, character name tied to the line, or phrase that's become common parlance
  - 🟡 = recognizable but short; risk only if we name the film/logo on the sign
  - 🔴 = long quote, or trademarked name/phrase — avoid or reword
- **Etsy potential**: ⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐ (gut estimate of search volume / giftability / bar-decor fit)
- Rule of thumb on all of these: put the quote + attributed character or a plausible bar/venue name — NOT the movie title or studio logo.

## IP Risk — Cheat Sheet

**Safe strategy for every sign:**
1. Put the quote in quotes
2. Attribute to the *character* (Jack Torrance, Ron Burgundy, Doc Holliday) — NOT the actor, NOT the film title
3. Never use a studio logo, movie poster imagery, or official font
4. If the character's name is itself a trademark (James Bond, 007), attribute to a descriptor instead ("— a certain British spy" / "— MI6's finest") or skip attribution

**Quotes to avoid entirely:**
- Anything Disney/Marvel/Star Wars
- Long quotes (>25 words) — more protectable under copyright
- Quotes that require the film's title to make sense

**Gray area to watch:**
- "McLovin" — name is distinctive, but being used as a quote is fine
- "The Dude" — no TM on that phrase itself
- Tombstone — public domain era setting + line-level quotes = low risk
