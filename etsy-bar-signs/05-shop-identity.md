# Shop Identity

The brand wrapping the collection. Goal: a name and voice that **sounds like a real speakeasy / whiskey bar supplier**, not a Printify reseller.

---

## Shop Name — candidates

Pick one. I've ranked them by my read of memorability, Etsy-search friendliness, and availability risk.

### Tier 1 — strongest

1. **Lloyd & Sons Sign Co.**
   *Nods to The Shining without naming it. Sounds like a 1940s hardware company. Google/Etsy check for availability before committing.*

2. **The Last Call Sign Co.**
   *Universal bar phrase. Evocative, not on-the-nose. Slightly generic but memorable.*

3. **Coughlin Supply Co.**
   *If you want to lead with the Cocktail connection (since that sign is your anchor). Feels like a real trade name.*

4. **The Back Bar**
   *Barman's term for the shelves behind the bar. Short, memorable, obviously drink-adjacent.*

### Tier 2 — decent

5. **Bourbon & Oak Sign Co.** — describes the aesthetic directly
6. **The Gin Joint Sign Co.** — Casablanca nod, slightly literal
7. **Cellar 12 / Cellar 18** — numbered cellar vibe, cryptic
8. **Neat Pour Signs** — bartender lingo
9. **The Burned Quote** — refers to wood-burn aesthetic

### Names to avoid
- Anything with "Movie" or "Film" in the name (sounds like merch)
- Anything with "Famous" or "Quotes" (generic)
- Anything with your name unless you like being attached publicly

### Availability check (do this before settling)

For each candidate, verify:
- `etsy.com/shop/<name>` — 404 is good
- `google.com/search?q="<name>"` — no existing bar-sign business with the name
- Social handles: `@<name>` on Instagram at minimum

---

## Recommended Choice: **Lloyd & Sons Sign Co.**

Reasoning — and why I'd pick this over the others:

- **Memorable + searchable.** Two-word brand with "Sign Co." suffix tells Etsy's algorithm exactly what you sell.
- **In-world voice.** Sounds like a supplier that would have *actually* made bar signs in 1945. This underwrites the rustic aesthetic.
- **Fan-connection without legal exposure.** Lloyd is a character name from The Shining. Character names aren't trademarked in the same way titles are — and "& Sons Sign Co." puts it clearly in supplier-company framing.
- **Room to grow.** If you expand to other rustic signs (not just bar), the brand still fits.

If **Lloyd & Sons** is taken or you don't want the horror-adjacent association, fall back to **The Last Call Sign Co.**

## Tagline (short line under shop name on Etsy)

Pick one (140-char Etsy limit):

1. *"Movie-quote bar signs. Rustic wood. For the home bar that takes itself a little too seriously."* (120 chars)
2. *"Bar signs, hand-lettered in the style of the movies you keep quoting."* (71 chars)
3. *"For the home bar, the man cave, and the one friend who won't shut up about Tombstone."* (86 chars)
4. *"Rustic wood signs. Whiskey-bar aesthetic. Quotes you've already been quoting for years."* (88 chars)

**My pick: #1.** Tells the buyer exactly what it is, and the "takes itself a little too seriously" line signals the tone is self-aware, not earnest farmhouse.

## Voice / Tone Guidelines (for every piece of copy)

- **Smart bartender, not fan account.** Never "OMG" or "obsessed." Always slightly dry.
- **Assume the reader gets the reference.** Don't over-explain jokes.
- **Short sentences.** Imperatives where possible.
- **No emoji in listings or bio** — it kills the aesthetic. Emoji OK in casual DMs.
- **"We"** not "I" — sounds more like a shop, even if it's just you.

## Logo / Wordmark Concept

For Etsy banner + packaging. Keep it cheap to produce — you don't need a full brand identity on day one.

Simply: the shop name set in **Cinzel Bold**, all caps, wide tracking (+200), on a dark walnut panel with a 1px gold border. That's it. Matches the design system. Works at any size.
