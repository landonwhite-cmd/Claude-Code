# Fulfillment: Printify vs Alternatives

> **Decision recorded.** We pivoted Wave 1 away from wood entirely — after seeing the user's existing Coughlin's Law sign, the anchor turned out to be a printed navy panel in a thin black frame. Wave 1 now ships via Printify's **Framed Poster** product (see `06-printify-product-spec.md`). Wood remains on the roadmap as the **Wave 2 premium tier** via an Etsy Manufacturing Partner. The analysis below is preserved for that Wave 2 evaluation.

---

**TL;DR:** If you want *truly* rustic wood (real stained wood, visible grain, possibly engraved/burned-look), Printify is **not ideal**. Their "wood" catalog is mostly **printed-on-wood panels** — decent, but not premium craftsman vibe. For the $50–$100 "high quality" tier, you probably want one of the alternatives below, or a hybrid strategy.

---

## Option A: Printify (what you originally asked about)

### What they actually offer for "wood"
- **Printed wood wall art / wood panels** — UV-printed onto birch or similar. Looks flat and printed. Visible grain depends on provider.
- **Wood-print signs (framed/unframed)** — again, printed, not engraved/burned
- No true burned, CNC-routed, or hand-stained rustic signs in their catalog

### Price math (wood print panel, ~11×14)
- Printify cost: ~$18–$24
- Shipping: ~$6–$10
- Etsy fees (transaction + payment + listing): ~$4 on a $50 sale
- **Your profit at $50 retail: ~$14–$20**
- At $75 retail: ~$40–$45

### Pros
- Fastest to launch (integrates directly with Etsy)
- Zero inventory, zero upfront cost
- Automated order forwarding

### Cons
- Aesthetic doesn't match "classy, rustic wood"
- Customers expecting wood-burn at $75 will leave bad reviews
- Thin differentiation from a thousand other Printify shops

---

## Option B: Etsy Manufacturing Partner (recommended for your goal)

Etsy lets you partner with a US-based maker who produces your signs to spec. You design, they make, they ship.

### How it works
1. Find a wood-sign maker on `etsy.com/manufacturing` or via direct outreach
2. Send them your designs (AI/SVG/PDF)
3. They produce on demand or in small batches
4. Sign a partnership agreement (Etsy allows this — not considered reselling)
5. You list, they fulfill, you pay them per-unit

### Typical unit cost (12×18" rustic wood w/ painted lettering, small US maker)
- Maker cost: $22–$35
- Shipping: maker handles, built into cost or flat-fee
- **Your profit at $75 retail: ~$35–$50**
- At $95 retail: ~$55–$70

### Pros
- **Actually rustic wood** — real stain, real grain, real craftsmanship
- Justifies $75–$100 pricing
- Strong reviews → better Etsy search ranking
- Differentiated from Printify-sea

### Cons
- Requires upfront outreach to find a reliable partner
- Longer production (3–7 days typical)
- If partner flakes, you have to scramble

### Finding partners
- Search Etsy itself for existing wood-sign makers — many will white-label
- Faire.com wholesale marketplace (filter: wood signs, made in USA)
- Direct cold email to small workshops (Instagram hashtag search: #woodsignshop #rusticwoodsigns)

## Option D: Hybrid — start with Printify, upgrade as you scale

1. **Launch on Printify** with the 10-sign Wave 1 — learn which designs sell
2. Keep prices at **$35–$55** (honest pricing for printed product)
3. Once you have sales data, move your winners to a **manufacturing partner** with upgraded product (real wood, $75–$100)
4. Offer both: "Classic Print" SKU and "Premium Rustic Wood" SKU on the same listing

### Why this is compelling
- Low-risk launch
- Sales data filters your designs before you invest in partner relationships
- Two-tier pricing captures budget and premium buyers

---

## Option E: Self-fulfill (buy a laser engraver / CNC)

- **Glowforge** (~$4k) or **xTool** (~$1.5–3k) — laser engravers that do rustic wood beautifully
- Unit cost: ~$5–$12 (wood blank + finish + packaging)
- **Profit at $75 retail: ~$55–$65**
- But: you're now running a workshop. Time, space, shipping logistics.

Only pursue if you actually want a side-business that's hands-on.
