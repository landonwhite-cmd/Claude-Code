# Shop Policies

Copy-paste these into Etsy → Shop Manager → Settings → Policies. Adjust the specifics (your location, your timezone) where noted.

---

## Shipping

```
PROCESSING TIME
Every sign is made to order. Production takes 3–5 business days before your order ships. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as it leaves our fulfillment partner.

SHIPPING TIME (after processing)
- United States: 3–7 business days via USPS or UPS
- Canada: 7–14 business days
- (International outside US/Canada: not currently offered — message us if interested)

SHIPPING COST
- United States: FREE shipping included on every sign
- Canada: flat $8 per sign

CUSTOMS / DUTIES
International buyers (Canada) are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes. We ship Delivered Duty Unpaid (DDU).

LOST OR DAMAGED IN TRANSIT
If your package arrives damaged or doesn't arrive at all, message us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We'll replace it, no questions.
```

---

## Returns & Exchanges

```
Because every sign is made to order, we don't accept returns for "changed my mind." Please double-check the size and quote before ordering.

We DO offer a full refund or free replacement if:
- The sign arrives damaged
- The sign shipped has a defect (printing error, wrong size, wrong sign)
- Your package is lost in transit

To request a refund or replacement:
1. Message us within 14 days of delivery
2. Include a photo of the issue
3. We'll sort it within 24 hours

Custom orders (quotes not listed in the shop) are final sale unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.
```

## Payment

```
We accept all payment methods supported by Etsy Payments: credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Etsy gift cards. Sales tax is calculated and collected by Etsy automatically.
```

## FAQ (optional — add to shop description or a separate "About" section)

```
IS THE WOOD REAL?
Yes. Every sign is printed on real birch or pine wood panels (depending on the design). It's not MDF or faux-wood paper — it's actual wood.

CAN I HANG IT OUTDOORS?
Not recommended. UV-printed wood holds up well indoors, but outdoor exposure will fade the ink within a season.

HOW DO I HANG IT?
Every sign ships with a pre-cut keyhole slot in the back — just hang on a single nail or screw. No extra hardware required.

DO YOU DO CUSTOM QUOTES?
Sometimes. Message us with the quote and we'll let you know if it fits our style. Custom orders are $20 more than the matching stock size and have a 2-week turnaround.

CAN I RETURN A GIFT?
Personal and gift orders both fall under our "made to order" policy — no returns unless damaged or defective. If you're buying a gift and aren't sure of the recipient's taste, message us before ordering and we'll help.

WHAT IF I DON'T SEE MY FAVORITE MOVIE QUOTE?
We add new quotes every few weeks. Send us yours — if enough buyers request the same quote, we'll add it to the shop.

DO YOU SHIP INTERNATIONALLY?
Currently US and Canada only. If you're elsewhere, message us — if you cover shipping, we can usually make it work.
```
